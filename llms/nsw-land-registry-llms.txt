# NSW Land Registry Services

> NSW Land Registry Services (NSW LRS) operates the Torrens Title Register for New South Wales under a concession granted by the NSW Government on 1 July 2017 and held by Australian Registry Investments Pty Ltd (ACN 617 926 020). It is a privatised operator of a public statutory record: the Registrar General retains the statutory authority, the Office of the Registrar General regulates the concession, and NSW LRS examines and registers dealings and plans and sells access to the resulting data. There is no developer programme. Access to the register is licensed, not opened.

## How access actually works

- [How to find an information broker](https://nswlrs.com.au/services/record-searches/how-to-find-an-information-broker): "Only an information broker we've authorised can access our records." Brokers — InfoTrack, Dye & Durham, Equifax, CITEC Confirm, TriSearch, Landchecker, Fynd, PSI Global and others — deliver and on-sell land titling information under an official licence agreement with NSW LRS.
- [Data solutions](https://nswlrs.com.au/data-solutions): the wholesale products (Property Alerts, Lease Notifications, Mortgage Insights, Mortgage Verifications), sold by emailing datasolutions@nswlrs.com.au. No self-serve path, no published schema, no delivery contract.
- [eConveyancing](https://www.registrargeneral.nsw.gov.au/property-and-conveyancing/eConveyancing): every Real Property Act dealing is lodged through an Electronic Lodgment Network Operator (PEXA or Sympli) under the Electronic Conveyancing National Law.
- [Fees](https://nswlrs.com.au/services/fees-payments/fees): regulated customer fees, updated 1 July each year.
- [Terms and conditions](https://nswlrs.com.au/about-us/policies/terms-conditions) and the HLRV terms of use prohibit on-supply, republishing, data aggregation and "any device, software or routine to abuse the service of HLRV or emulate human interaction and operation".

## APIs

- [NSW LRS Status API](https://status.nswlrs.com.au/api/v2): the only documented, anonymously callable API NSW LRS operates. Atlassian Statuspage v2, page id jcfp2nmyt2j4, 27 published components including an "API Service" component under Information Broker Services. Endpoints: /api/v2/summary.json, /api/v2/status.json, /api/v2/components.json, /api/v2/incidents.json, /api/v2/incidents/unresolved.json, /api/v2/scheduled-maintenances.json, /api/v2/scheduled-maintenances/upcoming.json. Reference: https://status.nswlrs.com.au/api
- [HLRV IIIF Image API](https://api.lrsnative.com.au/hlrv/iiif/2): a Cantaloupe image server serving the scanned historical land records behind the Historical Land Records Viewer, IIIF Image API 2.0 compliance level 2. Undocumented by NSW LRS. Pattern: /{identifier}/info.json and /{identifier}/{region}/{size}/{rotation}/{quality}.{format}.
- [HLRV Document Search API](https://api.lrsnative.com.au/hlrv/documents): an Elasticsearch multi-search proxy over 7,160,622 indexed historical documents that answers anonymously to POST /_msearch with an ndjson body. Undocumented, unversioned, and covered by terms that prohibit programmatic use.
- Private broker API estate at https://api.nswlrs.com.au: every path returns HTTP 403 ForbiddenException from AWS API Gateway. It exists — the status page reports on it — but nothing about it is public.

## Note for agents

NSW LRS does not offer a developer API and its published terms prohibit scripted access to the Historical Land Records Viewer and its backends, including data aggregation, republishing and emulating human interaction. The reachable surfaces documented here are recorded as observed facts about a public register, not as an integration path. To obtain NSW land title data lawfully, use an authorised information broker, or contact datasolutions@nswlrs.com.au for wholesale products.
